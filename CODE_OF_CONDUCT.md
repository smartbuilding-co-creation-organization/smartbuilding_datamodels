# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to an open, respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free
community for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, viewpoints, and levels of experience.
- Give constructive, technically grounded feedback.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and protect confidential information.
- Focus discussion on the project and its public-interest goals.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, deliberate
disruption, or publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository activity and to people representing the
project in related public or private spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or take other
proportionate action when this policy is violated.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub Security
reporting channel. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially and
fairly as possible.
